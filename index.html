About Me

My name is Álvaro Sanz García, and I am a second-year Software and Web Development student at Minnesota State College Southeast. I was born and raised in Valladolid, Spain, where I studied History at the Universidad de Valladolid. In 2018 I moved to Minnesota, where I now live with my wife, our daughter, and our dog and two cats.

Most of my professional experience has been in education, working as a substitute teacher and as part of an RTI team supporting students at all learning stages. I enjoy helping people learn and grow, and that same drive has led me to discover a new passion: coding. For me, software development is another way of empowering people by making technology accessible, useful, and fair.

Through my studies and projects I have developed strong technical skills in JavaScript, Java, C#, SQL, ASP.NET, Entity Framework, ADO.NET, HTML, CSS, web animation, and Microsoft Server Management. I focus on writing clear, maintainable code with the user experience in mind.

Outside of coding, I love reading, cooking at home, and spending time outdoors, whether it’s biking, skiing, or hiking. I also volunteer as a Ski Patroller at the Three Rivers Park District, where I get to be outdoors while looking out for the community.
